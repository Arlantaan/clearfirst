# Assignment: investigate Northbridge v. Caldera

You work for Northbridge Industries' lawyers in a fictional dispute over a failed ERP implementation (Project Keystone).

**The question:** did Caldera know the system was not ready for go-live, and tell Northbridge it was?

**Why it matters:** the contract caps Caldera's liability at the fees Northbridge paid, $4,187,500. The cap does not apply to gross negligence, wilful misconduct, or fraud. If Caldera knowingly misrepresented readiness, Northbridge can claim $9,127,500.

## What to do

- Inspect the documents rather than assuming the answer. Some are irrelevant; some contradict each other.
- Ground every factual claim in a document, page, and exact quotation. Keep evidence separate from interpretation.
- Do not guess when decisive evidence is missing or unreadable.
- Finish with a memo: the answer, a finding per checklist item with citations, and remaining gaps.

The case and every document are entirely fictional. This is not legal advice.
